This supplementary webpage presents experimental videos associated with the manuscript:

"Contact-Assured Industrial Manipulation via Closed-Loop Visuo-Tactile Cyber-Physical Coordination"

The videos demonstrate the proposed visuo-tactile closed-loop manipulation framework, including real-platform grasping experiments, adaptive gripper velocity regulation, and optical tactile sensing feedback.

Supplementary Experimental Videos
Supplementary Video 1
Closed-Loop Visuo-Tactile Grasping Experiment

This video demonstrates the real-platform closed-loop grasping experiment. The demonstration includes visuo-tactile perception, adaptive gripper velocity regulation, and contact-depth-based stopping strategy.

Supplementary Video 2
D-Sight Optical Tactile Sensing and Deformation Feedback

This video demonstrates the D-Sight optical tactile sensing process and tactile deformation feedback captured by the sensor. The acquired tactile information provides contact feedback for the proposed visuo-tactile closed-loop manipulation framework.

Experimental Platform

The experimental platform consists of:

  • UR5 collaborative robotic arm
  • OmniPicker two-finger adaptive gripper
  • Two D-Sight optical tactile sensors
  • Orbbec Femto Bolt RGB-D camera

The supplementary videos demonstrate the visuo-tactile closed-loop manipulation process, adaptive gripper velocity regulation, and tactile sensing feedback.
