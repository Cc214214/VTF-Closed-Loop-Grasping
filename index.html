This supplementary webpage presents experimental videos associated with the manuscript:

"Contact-Assured Industrial Manipulation via Closed-Loop Visuo-Tactile Cyber-Physical Coordination"

The supplementary videos demonstrate the proposed closed-loop visuo-tactile manipulation framework, which integrates visual perception, optical tactile sensing, contact-state inference, and adaptive gripper velocity regulation within a perception–decision–actuation loop. The demonstrations include real-platform grasping experiments and D-Sight optical tactile sensing feedback, showing how multimodal sensing information supports reliable contact-aware manipulation.

Supplementary Experimental Videos

Supplementary Video 1

Closed-Loop Visuo-Tactile Grasping Demonstration

This video demonstrates the real-platform closed-loop visuo-tactile grasping experiment proposed in this work. The experiment is conducted on an industrial manipulation platform consisting of a UR5 collaborative robotic arm, an OmniPicker two-finger adaptive gripper, an Orbbec Femto Bolt RGB-D camera, and two D-Sight optical tactile sensors.

During the grasping process, visual and tactile information are synchronously collected to estimate the contact state and regulate the gripper motion. The proposed framework enables adaptive gripper velocity regulation based on contact information and applies a contact-depth-based stopping strategy to achieve stable object grasping.

The video shows the complete manipulation procedure, including object observation, gripper approaching, contact-aware closing, adaptive velocity adjustment, and final object lifting.

Supplementary Video 2

D-Sight Optical Tactile Sensing and Deformation Feedback

This video demonstrates the D-Sight optical tactile sensing process and the tactile deformation feedback captured by the sensor. The D-Sight optical tactile sensors provide contact information through visual measurement of deformation occurring on the sensing surface.

The captured tactile information is used as an additional sensing modality alongside visual observation within the proposed visuo-tactile framework. The demonstration shows the sensor response during contact interaction and illustrates how tactile deformation feedback contributes to contact-state understanding.

Experimental Platform

The experimental platform consists of:

  • UR5 collaborative robotic arm
  • OmniPicker two-finger adaptive gripper
  • Two D-Sight optical tactile sensors
  • Orbbec Femto Bolt RGB-D camera

The experimental platform integrates robotic manipulation, RGB-D visual sensing, and optical tactile sensing to enable closed-loop visuo-tactile interaction. The supplementary videos demonstrate the complete manipulation process, adaptive gripper velocity regulation, and tactile sensing feedback within the proposed framework.
